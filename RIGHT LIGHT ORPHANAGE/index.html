WHO WE ARE

Right Light Gospel Orphanage

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to providing a loving, stable, and nurturing family environment for orphaned and vulnerable children in Nigeria. Our mission is to empower each child to overcome their circumstances and build a future filled with promise.

Through comprehensive care that includes education, healthcare, and emotional support, we light up the path for our children to become confident, compassionate, and resilient individuals.

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our work, donations, and how you can get involved.

Your donation directly supports the children's well-being. Approximately 85% goes to direct care programs including food, education, healthcare, and clothing. The remaining 15% covers essential administrative and operational costs to keep our home running safely and efficiently.
Yes, we are fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in Nigeria and comply with all regulations set by the Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development. Our commitment to transparency and accountability is paramount.
Absolutely! We welcome in-kind donations. Items that are most needed include non-perishable food items, children's clothing (new or gently used), educational toys, and school supplies. Please call us or use the contact form to arrange a drop-off time.
We are grateful for our volunteers! The process involves filling out an application form, a background check for the safety of our children, and an orientation session. We have roles for tutoring, mentorship, organizing activities, and more. Please visit our contact page to express your interest.
To protect the privacy and routine of our children, we encourage scheduled visits. Please contact us in advance to arrange a suitable time. This ensures we can host you properly without disrupting the children's daily activities and education.
